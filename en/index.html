Famous From Here

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Famous From Here ("the App") is a mobile application that helps you discover notable people associated with a place. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not require an account. We do not operate our own servers to store your personal profile, search history, or location history. We do not sell your information, and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs inside the App.

2. Information Processed on Your Device

When you use the App, the following may be processed locally on your device:

  • Place names you type into the search field
  • Your device location, only if you choose "Use current location"
  • App language preferences based on your device settings

This information is used solely to perform the search you requested. It is not saved by the App after the search completes unless your operating system keeps it in a text field or permission cache.

3. Location Information

If you tap "Use current location," the App requests permission to access your device's location while the App is in use. Location data is used only to determine nearby place names through your device's reverse geocoding service (for example, Apple or Google location services). The App does not continuously track your location in the background.

You can deny location permission and still use the App by entering a place name manually. You can change this permission at any time in your device settings.

4. Third-Party Services

The App communicates with external services to show results:

  • Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation): When you search, the App sends place names to the Wikipedia API to retrieve public article and category information. Wikipedia may process these requests according to its own privacy practices.
  • Device location services: When you use current location, reverse geocoding is handled by your device's platform provider. That provider may process location data under its own terms and privacy policy.

When you open a person's Wikipedia page from the App, you leave the App and are subject to Wikipedia's website policies.

5. Data Retention

The App developer does not receive or store your searches or location on developer-owned servers. Any temporary processing happens on your device or with the third-party services described above.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the latest version. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: ryuhosoy@icloud.com
